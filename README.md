### Featured projects

**[YouTube Transcript Scraper](https://github.com/lmy-dawson/youtube-transcript-scraper)** · Python, asyncio, httpx · [live](https://apify.com/teamsasuke/youtube-transcript-scraper)
Extracts transcripts from any YouTube video, channel, playlist or search as plain text, timestamped segments, SRT, WebVTT or chunks ready for embedding. Gets past YouTube's bot blocking by pinning each worker to its own proxy session and retrying the player call and caption download as one unit.

**[App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper](https://github.com/lmy-dawson/app-reviews-scraper)** · Python, httpx · [live](https://apify.com/teamsasuke/app-reviews-scraper)
Collects reviews from both app stores into a single schema. Handles Apple's per-address rate limits with adaptive pacing and proxy-session rotation, and steps over the store's intermittent gaps instead of stopping early.

**[Florida Property Records](https://github.com/lmy-dawson/florida-property-records)** · Python, httpx, openpyxl · [live](https://apify.com/teamsasuke/florida-property-records)
Normalises 858,000 public parcel records from two counties, published in different formats, into one schema with owner, tax, sale and lot-size fields.

**[Visa Monitor Bot](https://github.com/lmy-dawson/VisaMonitorBot)** · Python, Docker
Watches embassy visa-appointment pages and sends Telegram and WhatsApp alerts the moment a slot opens.
